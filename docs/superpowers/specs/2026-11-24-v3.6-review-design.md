# ChessBot 3.6 review design

ChessBot 3.6 builds on the 3.5 Practice deck and finished-game review at commit `704db0a`. Opening recognition and review stay offline. The review keeps the bundled full-network Stockfish, and works for both Chess.com archives and pasted PGNs.

## Book moves

The extension bundles a reproducible book from the CC0 Lichess opening lines and frequent continuations from strong rated games. Look up the played UCI move from its exact opening position so ordinary transpositions work. A match can display as Book when the engine gives it Best, Excellent, or Good with sufficient evidence. Forced moves, terminal moves, and any missed-mate, uncertain, or tactical grade keep the engine label. The coach card identifies the line, and the move list, board badge, and summary use the same Book label. Accuracy, centipawn loss, Decision Quality, and practice drills continue to use the underlying evaluation.

## Engine and grades

Only a complete MultiPV iteration establishes a same-position comparison. A capped recheck that cannot verify a grade leaves it marked uncertain at the depth the engine completed. Compare any proposed search policy change to 3.5 on paired positions and a deeper reference using the existing benchmark harness. Keep the existing default budget unless there is evidence for changing it.

Miss is useful coaching context, and loses priority to Blunder above the existing 20-point severity boundary. The summary exposes how many decisions each score covers and how many grades stayed uncertain.

## Ratings

Keep the game-record or PGN rating when supplied and describe its source. Evaluate any single-game estimate against calendar holdouts and time-class baselines, with error by sample size and rating band. A user-facing estimate would require independent evidence that the model is accurate enough to interpret, including a prediction interval and validation on the platform where it appears.
