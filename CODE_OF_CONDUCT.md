# Code of conduct

ChessBot follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, or discussions is expected to uphold it.

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience level or background.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Harassment, insults, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer, [@FrankieBiz](https://github.com/FrankieBiz), through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may remove comments or block contributors who break these rules.
